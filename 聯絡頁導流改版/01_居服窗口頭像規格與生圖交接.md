# 居服窗口頭像：使用規格與生圖交接

> 對應區塊：`/contact` 的 `#homecare` → `img.homecare-contact_photo`
> 狀態：2026-09-10 Terris 已提供既有照片，**§2 的生圖 prompt 僅作為未來補圖／變體用的備份**

---

## 1. 照片使用規格（提供的照片與未來任何替換都適用）

| 項目 | 規格 |
|---|---|
| 版面比例 | 卡片 `2/3`；頭像框 `1/1.15`（`.homecare-contact_portrait`） |
| 實際顯示尺寸 | 約 **384 × 442 px**（卡片 `width: 24rem`，`object-fit: cover`） |
| 建議來源尺寸 | 短邊 ≥ 768px 即滿足 2x；1024 × 1178 以上有餘裕，**不需要跑 upscaler** |
| 構圖 | 頭頂距上緣 8–12%、眼睛在上 1/3、下緣收在胸口；不要有手部入鏡 |
| 下半部 | 卡片下方是半透明白色資訊層（`--neutral--white-50` ＋ blur 5px），**胸口以下不要有重要細節** |
| 背景 | Jubo Signature Teal `#00B2C0` 平塗，與 5 位客戶經理的頭像一致 |
| 影像內容 | 不得出現任何文字、Jubo logo、名牌、識別證 |
| alt text | 「Jubo 居服顧問窗口示意形象」——**不要寫人名**，避免誤導 |
| 檔名建議 | `jubo-homecare-window.jpg`（或 `.webp`） |

## 2. 生圖 prompt（備份，未來要補變體時用）

交給支援生圖的工具（Codex／ChatGPT 影像、Midjourney、Nano Banana）。
依 `jubo-images` skill 的品牌規範收斂：不生成 logo 與文字、去 AI 感關鍵字必加。

```
# TASK
Generate a portrait "contact window" avatar for the Jubo (智齡科技) website.
Output PNG, 1024 x 1536 (2:3 portrait), no watermark, no text anywhere in the image.

# BACKGROUND CONTEXT
- Jubo / 智齡科技 is a Taiwanese care-technology company selling care-management
  software to residential care institutions (住宿型機構), day-care centres (日照中心),
  and home-care service units (居家服務單位／居服單位).
- The image goes on jubo-health.com/contact, in a card placed under the hero and ABOVE
  the grid of five real regional account managers.
- Business problem: home-care units skip the enquiry form and contact the
  residential/day-care account managers directly, which routes them to the wrong team.
  The card must give the same "a real person is waiting for you" reassurance as the
  account-manager cards, while its only call to action is a form. Warm, competent,
  approachable — someone you would happily write to, not a salesperson chasing a call.
- The person is a REPRESENTATIVE WINDOW, not a specific real employee. Do not imitate
  or resemble any identifiable real person. No brand logo, no badge text, no lettering.
- Consistency: the existing five account-manager headshots are studio portraits on a
  FLAT SOLID TEAL background, framed from just above the head to mid-chest, soft even
  studio light, natural unretouched skin, calm closed-mouth smile, slight three-quarter
  turn toward camera. The new avatar must sit beside them without looking off-source.

# SUBJECT
- One Taiwanese / East Asian person. Female, 32-38 (matches the real demographic of
  Taiwan home-care supervisors). Alternate: male, 30-40, same styling.
- Role read: home-care consultant at a care-technology company — approachable
  professional, social-worker energy, not corporate sales.
- Expression: candid, warm, natural closed-mouth smile, genuine and slightly
  asymmetric. Not a stock-photo grin, not a stiff ID-photo stare.
- Wardrobe: clean well-fitted smart casual — plain knit top / cotton or linen shirt.
  NO logo, print, lettering, lanyard or badge. Avoid medical scrubs and lab coats.
- Hair natural dark, tidy. Minimal jewellery, light natural make-up.

# BACKGROUND
- Flat solid Jubo Signature Teal #00B2C0, edge to edge, no gradient, no vignette,
  no pattern, no props. A very soft contact shadow at the silhouette is acceptable.

# COMPOSITION AND CROP (the card crops this image)
- Vertical portrait, subject centred, slight three-quarter turn, head upright.
- Top of head 8-12% down from the top edge; eye line in the upper third;
  frame ends mid-chest. No hands, no objects.
- Final crop is centre 1:1.15 (w:h) and the bottom ~35% of the card is covered by a
  translucent white panel — keep the lower frame visually quiet.
- Leave teal margin left and right of the shoulders so the crop never clips the silhouette.

# LIGHT, COLOUR, REALISM
- Soft even slightly-bright studio light from front-left, large soft source, gentle
  natural shadows preserved, low contrast, no rim light, no coloured gels.
- Natural realistic skin tone; the teal must not tint the skin.
- shot on Canon EOS R5, 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field, realistic optical rendering,
  natural skin texture with visible pores and fine lines, realistic hair detail,
  subtle film grain, authentic fabric texture, no retouching.

# NEGATIVE PROMPT
any text, letters, numbers, watermark, signature, logo, brand mark, name badge, ID card,
lanyard, plastic skin, waxy over-smooth skin, airbrushed, retouched, CGI, 3D render,
video-game render, uncanny valley, doll-like, symmetrical perfect face, fake perfect
teeth, mannequin, stock-photo look, over-saturated, over-sharpened, HDR halo, artificial
glow on skin, neon, glowing bubbles or particles on the subject, data-flow lines,
holograms, anime, cartoon, Western / Caucasian / non-Asian faces, hospital or clinical
setting, white lab coat, medical scrubs, stethoscope, office background, bookshelf,
window, gradient backdrop, purple as a dominant colour, business suit, hands in frame,
multiple people, children, elderly subject, deformed hands or ears, extra fingers,
crooked glasses, crooked shoulders, tilted horizon

# SELF-CHECK
1. No text, logo or badge anywhere.  2. Background flat #00B2C0 edge to edge.
3. Head-top margin 8-12%, eye line upper third, frame ends mid-chest, no hands.
4. Real skin texture, non-symmetrical face, candid expression.
5. Taiwanese / East Asian features; plain clothing, no lanyard.
6. Ears, eyes, shoulders, hairline anatomically correct.
7. Reads as a care-technology consultant — not a nurse, doctor or salesperson.
```

## 3. 出圖後檢查（`jubo-images` skill 的 image-qa 疊加層）

- 情境正確：不是醫院、不是科技 showroom
- 無第三方 logo、無生成文字、無捏造 UI
- 去 AI 感：皮膚有毛孔紋理、光線有自然陰影、表情不僵、無蠟感
- 縮到手機大小看 3 秒，覺得「假／像 AI」就重做
- 1:1.15 中央裁切後輪廓沒有被切到
